About the Practice

Begin your day with intention and clarity. This gentle morning flow is designed to awaken the body softly, linking breath with mindful movement. We will focus on opening the chest, stretching the spine, and grounding the mind before the day begins.

Expect a blend of deep, restorative holds and fluid transitions. Perfect for all levels, especially those looking to cultivate a sense of inner calm and sustained energy throughout their morning.
